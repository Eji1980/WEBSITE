Does this sound familiar?

“How do you lead when you are struggling yourself?”

It's the question leaders ask when they're drained, spread too thin and still expected to be everything for everyone. Most try to push through: faster replies, later evenings, a steady face in every meeting. But pushing harder doesn't refill anything, and your team feels it even when you think you're hiding it. Leading from full starts with the most important person on your team, you.

  • You are everything for everyone, and always last on your own list.
  • It feels easier to do it yourself than to hand it over.
  • You say yes when you mean no, then feel guilty or resentful.
  • You keep a steady face, so nobody sees you are running on empty.
  • You wonder: is it me, or is it the job?

None of this makes you a weak leader. It often means you care and take responsibility seriously. Every committed leader has limits, and honouring them is part of leading well.

What would you like more of?

Clearer priorities, shared responsibility, trust in yourself and room to breathe, at work and beyond it.

Questions you might have

I have no time. Is coaching realistic?

Sessions are online and focused. You can choose a single 90-minute session, or six sessions at your own pace.

What's the difference between a masterclass and coaching?

In a masterclass, you learn the ALIGN method with a group. In coaching, you work 1:1 on your own situation, you set the agenda, and you find your own way forward.

What if the problem is my organisation, not me?

That is often part of it. A new perspective won't fix too much work or too little support. We look at what is yours to change, and how to raise the rest.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.
