The method

More tools won't fix this. A stronger anchor will.

Most leadership development is horizontal. It adds skills, frameworks and knowledge. They matter, and I spent twenty years teaching them. But the leaders I met who were struggling rarely lacked tools. They had lost touch with what anchors them: who they are, what matters to them, and the quiet inner guidance that tells them when something is off.

That's the vertical side of leadership, and it's where my work begins. When the two come together, something shifts. You stay calm in chaos. You hear your own inner compass again. And you lead from full instead of running on empty.

That's what The Ownership Reset Method is built on. Own more: your direction, your energy and your life. Carry less: everything that was never yours to carry. Five ALIGN steps take you there, while you keep leading.

  • AAcknowledgeWhere you are now, and what brought you here.
  • LLocateWhat you really want, why it matters, and how you'll know it's working.
  • IInvestigateThe beliefs, patterns and obstacles in your way, and new ways to see them.
  • GGenerateThe tools you need, and a personal plan you can follow.
  • NNormalizeTry it in real life, adjust, and keep practising until it's yours.

My story

I know what it's like to carry everything.

I learned, the hard way, the difference between carrying everything and leading from full.

I grew up in Nigeria, with a Swedish mother and a Nigerian father, and spent my school years at a boarding school. At eighteen I moved to Sweden. I went to university, started my career as a programmer in Stockholm's tech industry, and soon moved into leadership.

For more than twenty years I led teams and organisations: as a manager, Agile coach, Scrum Master, product owner and consultant. I built and improved about fifteen to twenty teams, always well-being first, and most recently built a five-team digital organisation at a bank.

In 2016 my husband Fredrik died. I kept leading, and raised our two children on my own. Grief took me deep into psychology, spirituality and what really changes people. I learned, the hard way, the difference between carrying everything and leading from full.

In 2025 I left the bank to train as a transformational life coach, alongside a year of integrative psychology. Today I bring both worlds together: the practical experience of two decades in leadership, and the inner work that makes change last.
