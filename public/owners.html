Somewhere along the way, ownership started to mean carrying everything: every decision, every problem, every person's mood. That isn't ownership. It's over-responsibility, and it's why you can't switch off even when the business is fine.

Real ownership is lighter.You own your direction, your energy and your life, and you set down the rest.

Does this sound familiar?

“The business is doing fine. So why am I this tired?”

It's the question owners ask late at night, phone still in hand. So you push harder: faster replies, later evenings, a calm face for the team. But pushing harder doesn't fix it.

  • You can't stop thinking about work, even at night and at weekends.
  • You say yes when you mean no, then feel guilty or resentful.
  • It's quicker to do it yourself, so every problem still ends up on your desk.
  • Your body has started sending signals you keep ignoring.
  • You've quietly wondered: should I close the business and get a job?

None of this means you're a weak leader. It means you've been carrying what was never yours.

The real cost

What it's costing you, and the business

A drained owner makes slower decisions, misses what the team needs, and keeps good people waiting until they leave. The business can't grow past what you can personally hold. And the people at home get what's left at the end of the day.

Why it keeps happening

Why the usual advice doesn't stick

You've read the delegation tips and the boundary scripts. They work for a week. Then the old pattern wins, because the problem isn't a lack of tools. It's a belief underneath them: if I don't carry it, no one will.

The Ownership Reset works on both levels: the practical tools for switching off, holding boundaries and letting your team own more, and the inner beliefs that keep pulling you back.

Questions you might have

Is this burnout? Can I recover while still working?

The masterclass and coaching are built for owners who are still running their business. If you're dealing with panic attacks or severe burnout, medical or therapeutic help may be the right first step, and this can work alongside it.

I can't take time off. Is this realistic?

Yes. Everything is online and focused: one live masterclass, one 90-minute session, or six sessions at your own pace.

Should I close the business and get a job?

Maybe, but decide from a rested place, not an exhausted one. Many owners find they need a different way of leading, not a different business.

What if the problem is the business, not me?

That's often part of it. A new perspective won't fix too much work or too few people. We look at what's yours to change, and how to raise the rest.

Coaching or the masterclass?

The masterclass if you want a clear method and other owners around you. Coaching if your question is about your own situation. Not sure? Book the free conversation.

Is this therapy?

No. This is teaching, mentoring and coaching. It works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.
