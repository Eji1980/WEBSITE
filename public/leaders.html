How it works

Five steps, from the inside out

Frameworks change the structure. The Ownership Reset also changes what pulls you and your leaders back in when the pressure is on. In your real work, not on top of it.

  1. AAcknowledge

    See what really circles back to you, and why.

  2. LLocate

    Define what ownership should look like, for you and your leaders.

  3. IInvestigate

    Uncover the beliefs and patterns that pull you back in.

  4. GGenerate

    Build the tools, agreements and plan to hand over for real.

  5. NNormalize

    Practise in real work until stepping back is the new normal.

Questions you might have

Is this coaching or a programme?

The Ownership Reset is my own teaching method. 1:1 coaching is separate: it follows the Wayfinder method and ICF ethics, and you set the agenda.

Can I bring my own leaders?

Yes, that's part of the idea: the work happens in you and in your leaders at the same time. We'll talk about what fits in the free conversation.

Is this therapy?

No. This is teaching, mentoring and coaching. It works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.
