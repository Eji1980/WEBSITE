Does this sound familiar?

“Do you care too much to lead well?”

It's the question caring leaders ask late in the evening. So you push harder: faster replies, later nights, a steady face. But pushing harder doesn't refill anything.

  • You feel responsible for everything, and everyone's emotions.
  • It's easier to do it yourself than to hand it over, so you pick up what slips.
  • You say yes when you mean no, then feel guilty or resentful.
  • You're home, but your head is still at work.
  • You wonder: is it me, or is it the job? Should I even be a leader?

None of this makes you a weak leader. Your care is a strength. It just needs somewhere to go other than your own shoulders.

Questions you might have

Which one should I choose, coaching or a masterclass?

Coaching if your question is about your own life, team or organisation. The Ownership Reset Masterclass if you want a clear method and other leaders around you. Not sure? Book the free conversation.

I can't take time off. Is this realistic?

Yes. Both are designed for leaders who are still leading. Sessions are online and focused: one 90-minute session, six sessions at your own pace, or a single live masterclass.

What if the problem is my organisation, not me?

That's often part of it. A new perspective won't fix too much work or too little support. We look at what's yours to change, and how to raise the rest.

Will coaching tell me what to do?

No. Coaching isn't advice. I listen and ask questions so you can see clearly and decide for yourself. If you want a structured method, that's what The Ownership Reset Masterclass is for.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.
