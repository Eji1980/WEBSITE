It isn't a knowledge problem

You've already tried the sensible things.

  • Delegation
  • Decision rights
  • A reorg
  • Leadership training
  • Time management
  • A mindfulness app
  • A long holiday
  • Working harder

They changed the structure, or gave you a short break. They didn't change what happens under pressure.

When it gets urgent, something pulls you back in, and you run on empty to keep it all going. Something holds your leaders back too. That pull lives beneath the org chart and the to-do list, in you and in them. No framework or app touches it. That's where The Ownership Reset starts.

How it works

One reset. Two levels at once.

Most leadership development adds tools. The Ownership Reset works beneath them: on how you lead yourself, and on how your leaders own their part. In your real work, not on top of it.

Today

Every decision, problem and deadline runs through you.

After The Ownership Reset

Your leaders are aligned on shared goals and own their part. You lead them, and the work only you can do.

Questions

Before you book.

Can my company pay?

Most leaders' companies do: it's leadership development for you and your team. I'll help you make the case to your manager.

What if the organisation is the real problem?

Some of it may be. We start with what's yours to change, in you and in your team. I won't promise to fix the mandate above you, but you'll be much clearer about what to ask for.

I don't have time for this.

That's usually the clearest sign it's time. The work happens in your real week, on real decisions, not on top of it.

Is this coaching or a programme?

The Ownership Reset is my programme for you and your leaders. 1:1 coaching is separate: it follows the Wayfinder method and ICF ethics, and you set the agenda.

Is this therapy?

No. This is teaching, mentoring and coaching. It works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.
