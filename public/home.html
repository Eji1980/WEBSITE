Hi, I'm Eji

For twenty years I led teams and organisations in tech. Then life asked me to learn a different kind of leadership: the kind that starts inside.

Losing my husband Fredrik, and raising our two children on my own while I kept leading, taught me the difference between carrying everything and leading from full. Today I bring both worlds together: two decades of practical leadership, and the inner work that makes change last.
