My approach

The anchor first. Then the tools.

Most development works from the outside in: more skills, more frameworks. They matter, and we use them. But the people I work with rarely lack tools. They've lost touch with what anchors them. Today I bring both together: two decades of practical leadership, and the inner work that makes change last.

Questions

Before you book.

What is coaching, and what happens in a session?

You bring what's on your mind. I listen, ask, and help you see it from new angles, so you find your own answers and your next step. You set the agenda.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching looks forward and works alongside medical or therapeutic support, not instead of it.

Do I need to be a manager?

No. If you lead, at work, in a company of your own, or in your own life, and feel you've drifted from yourself, this is for you.

English or Swedish?

Either. All sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are.

What's the difference from The Ownership Reset?

Coaching is just for you, and you set the agenda. The Ownership Reset is my leadership programme for you and your leadership team. Read more about it.
